## 1.0.0 (2026-08-26)

First release, shipped alongside `utility-figjam-board` and `utility-figjam-harvest` as the run step of the FigJam loop.

### Contract established
- Four roles named before the session; Facilitator and Decider must differ
- Five beats per section: frame, silent write, cluster, discuss, close
- Final 10 minutes reserved for decisions and actions, taken out of the budget first
- Votes cast on clusters, counts written to the board
- Refusal: will not proceed without an identified Decider
