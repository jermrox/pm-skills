## 1.0.0 (2026-08-26)

First release, shipped alongside `utility-figjam-board` and `utility-figjam-workshop` as the harvest step of the FigJam loop.

### Contract established
- Evidence rule: verbatim quotes, counts not consensus, named gaps, no invented content
- Cluster discipline: labeled clusters keep the room's label; spatial proximity is never promoted to a theme
- Both `get_figjam` and `get_screenshot` required, because structure and layout each carry meaning the other loses
- Refusal: an empty section is reported as a finding, never filled
